# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Tessero to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
background, identity, experience or level of knowledge. We pledge to make
participation harassment-free and to act in ways that build an open,
diverse and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback about work, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming.
- Credit others' work.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (repository, issue tracker, chat,
events) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. All
reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers who are
involved in a report recuse themselves.

Maintainers may respond with a private warning, a public warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban, in proportion to the behaviour and its impact. They
explain their decision to the people involved.
